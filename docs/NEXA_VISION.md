# NEXA — Vision

## Core Concept

NEXA is a **persistent simulated world** that continues evolving independently of individual audience commands. It is not a normal game with scripted missions.

## Architecture

NEXA consists of three main layers:

1. **World Engine** — Simulation of economy, politics, factions, population, events, and consequences.
2. **AI Engine** — NIA (character AI), AI Director (narrative control), memory, relationships, and mystery systems.
3. **Presentation Layer** — Unreal Engine 5.8 for high-fidelity visuals, cinematic camera, audio, and UI.

## Audience Experience

Viewers can:
- Watch the world evolve in real time.
- Ask NIA questions.
- Issue commands that affect the simulation.
- Investigate events and mysteries.
- Vote on decisions.
- Build reputation and relationships.
- Discover long-form mysteries.
- See consequences unfold over time.

## First Playable Scope

- 1 city
- 1 district
- 20–30 major locations
- 100–500 simulated citizens
- 5 factions
- 10–20 businesses
- 1 government
- 1 media organization
- 1 major mystery
- 1 NIA
- 1 AI Director
- 1 audience command system

## Success Criteria

- The world persists across sessions.
- Events produce logical consequences.
- NIA remembers and references past events.
- Audience commands materially affect the simulation.
- The presentation layer delivers high-quality graphics and cinematic storytelling.
